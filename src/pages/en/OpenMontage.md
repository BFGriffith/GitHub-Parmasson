> `OpenMontage` is a free and open‑source Agentic video production tool
* [GitHub repository](https://github.com/calesthio/OpenMontage)

## FEATURES
- OpenMontage can start from a YouTube video, Short, Reel, TikTok, or local clip and turn it into a grounded production plan…
- works with Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf, Codex, and more…
